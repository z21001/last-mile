**Last Mile** is a runner through a city that has fallen to the dead. The last evacuation chopper leaves from the airfield 5 km away, and the horde is at your back: jump wrecked cars, slide under fallen billboards, shoot and stomp the zombies in your way, and throw molotovs into the crowds. Every hit lets the horde close in; if it catches you, the run is over.

- **Five zones, five bosses**: burning suburbs, downtown ruins, a dead highway, toxic docks and the airfield. Near the end of each zone a boss blocks the road and hurls barrels (jump) and car doors (slide); shoot its glowing heart while the horde closes in.
- **Rescue survivors**: people waving for help join your convoy, run behind you and fire at the dead. When you are hit, the last survivor shields you instead.
- **Safe houses**: after each boss pick one of three perks: a sawn-off shotgun, a stray dog, steel-toe boots, parkour, fire starter and more.
- **Win or go endless**: reach the chopper to evacuate, then keep running in endless mode for a high score.

Controls: **Space / W** jump (again in mid-air to double jump) · **S / Down** slide · **mouse + click** aim and shoot · **E / right click** molotov · **P** pause · **M** mute · **R** restart.

Plain HTML5 canvas and ES modules, no libraries, no assets: every sprite and sound is generated in code.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080
```
