**Last Mile** is an endless runner through a city that has fallen to the dead. The horde is at your back: jump wrecked cars, slide under fallen billboards, shoot and stomp the zombies in your way, and throw molotovs into the crowds. Every hit lets the horde close in, and the run ends when it catches you. Every 1000 m a safe house lets you pick one of three perks (a sawn-off shotgun, a stray dog, steel-toe boots, parkour and more) as the city changes from burning suburbs to ruined downtown, a dead highway and toxic docks.

Controls: **Space / W** jump (again in mid-air to double jump) · **S / Down** slide · **mouse + click** aim and shoot · **E / right click** molotov · **P** pause · **M** mute · **R** restart.

Plain HTML5 canvas and ES modules, no libraries, no assets: every sprite and sound is generated in code.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080
```
